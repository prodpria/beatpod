BEATPOD STORE - THE SIMPLE VERSION

ONE-TIME SETUP (about 10 minutes, easiest on a computer)
1. Go to github.com and make a free account.
2. Click "+" (top right) > "New repository". Name it: beatpod
   Make it Public. Click "Create repository".
3. Click "uploading an existing file". Unzip pria-beat-store.zip and drag
   in index.html and the beats folder. Click "Commit changes".
4. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" pick "main", then Save.
   After a minute your store is live at:
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/beatpod/

ADDING A BEAT (1 minute, works on your phone)
1. Rename your tagged preview MP3 like this:
   Night Drive - 140 - B min - trap.mp3
2. In your beatpod repository, open the beats folder >
   "Add file" > "Upload files" > pick the MP3 > "Commit changes".
That's it. It shows up on the pod within a few minutes. No code.

REMOVING OR SELLING A BEAT
- Remove: open the file in the beats folder > "..." > Delete file.
- Sold exclusive: delete it, or upload a copy with "- SOLD" at the end
  of the name and delete the old one. It stays listed but can't be bought.

PRICES, EMAIL, PAYMENT LINKS
Only if you want to change them: open index.html > pencil icon >
find "YOUR STORE" near the top.

USING YOUR OWN DOMAIN LATER (like store.prodpria.com)
Open index.html, find  github: ""  and fill in  github: "YOUR-USERNAME/beatpod"
